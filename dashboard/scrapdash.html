How the numbers are made

Scraps per dismantle: 6 / 18 / 54 / 162 / 486 / 1458 by rarity, audited against 17.3 million real dismantles. Market listings are always at 100% durability, so no wear adjustment applies.

Scrap value = scraps × the scrap price (the lowest sell order). That is the whole calculation: no tax is added or removed on either side, and the gear price is compared exactly as the market shows it. At best bid is the same multiplication with the highest buy order, for reference.

Read-only: one order-book read per interval, with or without an API key, no browser session, no item-market crawl. The item market is yours to sweep by hand.
