Using the game's recipe table; no overrides stored.

The Craft Desk ships the game's recipe table: the tier's scrap value in scraps (6 to 1458) plus a steel fee (1 to 32, doubled when the slot is chosen). A recipe typed on the desk overrides it for that item and is kept on this browser only. The craft ledger is exported and imported from the desk itself.
